# Festival prep pack

## Inputs
Event, date, venue, indoor/outdoor, overnight or not, transport, artists you want to see. Ask once for what's missing.

## Research
1. From the official site/socials: timetable, banned items, entry/re-entry rules, lockers, shuttles, rain policy.
2. Weather for the date (if more than a week away, use seasonal averages and mark "not a forecast").
3. Put the source and check date next to every item. Mark anything unconfirmed as "unconfirmed".

## Output (three parts)
1. **Packing checklist** with checkboxes: essentials (ticket, ID, payment, power bank) / weather / outdoor & camping / hygiene & meds (personal meds are your call) / banned-item check. Use a spreadsheet if available.
2. **Day timeline** around the stages you want: travel, meals, rest, water and restroom breaks. Ask which act wins when sets overlap.
3. **Stamina basics**: sleep the night before, water intervals, shade breaks, rotating out of the front standing area. General guidance only — medical questions belong to a doctor.

## Wrap-up
If a calendar is connected, ask once whether to add the timeline as events. Report briefly: what was made, unconfirmed items, decisions needed.
