# Short-form planner

## 1. Research the problem before the script
For the topic, collect up to 10 items for each of the 8 lists below in `research.md`, then pick the single most relatable one as this episode's angle. Use recent real comments (Reddit, X, YouTube, TikTok) — not your own guesses.
1. Problems people keep running into
2. Frustrations and complaints
3. The result they actually want
4. Questions they ask most
5. Common mistakes
6. Why they hesitate ("this isn't for me")
7. The feeling behind the problem
8. The exact plain words they use (reuse them in captions)

## 2. Structure (always this order)
| Part | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hook 1 | 0–2s | Break a belief. Visual hook (motion, before/after) and a one-line text hook at the same time |
| Hook 2 | 2–3s | A stronger twist on hook 1. This is where people decide to stay |
| Body | 3s–end | Empathy + genuinely useful info: the problem, the mistake, the new angle. No filler |
| CTA | last | Exactly one next action, answering the question the hook raised |

Pick ONE CTA by goal: follow (give a concrete reason), comment (one signature keyword), save (why they'll come back), share (who exactly to send it to), sell (a natural next step, e.g. "link in bio").

## 3. Script and shot list
Finish the script, then list shots as `start–end | part | on screen | line/caption | audio | source`. Keep sources in internal notes, not the public caption.

## 4. QA
Check 9:16 safe areas (bottom ~1/5 is covered by platform UI), cover title, and tags. After posting, log 0–3s drop-off and pre-CTA drop-off from the retention graph and use it for the next hook.
